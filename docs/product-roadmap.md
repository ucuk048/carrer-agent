# Product Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation

- PostgreSQL schema.
- Candidate profile and resume ingestion.
- Job ingestion from one permitted API/RSS source.
- Deterministic scoring.
- Approval inbox.
- Audit log.

## Phase 2 — Intelligence

- LLM structured extraction with JSON schema.
- Resume evidence bank and semantic retrieval.
- Employer and job risk signals.
- Tailored resume variants.
- Screening question draft generator.
- Golden dataset and quality evaluation.

## Phase 3 — Execution assistance

- Browser-assisted prefill on permitted application surfaces.
- Screenshot/state capture before approval.
- Human confirmation for every final submission.
- Follow-up scheduling and interview preparation.

## Phase 4 — Career operating system

- Weekly career review.
- Skill-gap prioritization.
- Portfolio/project recommendations.
- Interview outcome learning loop.
- Analytics for conversion rate, response rate, and time-to-interview.
